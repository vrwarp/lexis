Translate a book

Upload an EPUB and a target language. The engine extracts the book, builds a glossary, then drafts, audits, critiques, polishes and verifies every chapter with specialized agents — several chapters at a time — and checks in with you before packaging the translated EPUB.

The strong model does the translating. The cheaper one does everything else.
